The built-in updater is gone. Husk no longer downloads or installs anything by itself, and
the "install unknown apps" permission is no longer requested. Update from F-Droid, or install
a new APK with adb.

New: the camera side can be switched over HTTP with /set?front=0|1, and /flags now reports the
selected side in a "front" field. No cable and no adb needed.

The prebuilt APK has been removed from the source tree; releases carry it instead.
